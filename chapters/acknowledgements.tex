The author thanks the author's advisor, Dr.\ Javier Alejandro Ch\'avez-Dom\'inguez, for discussions of some of the results of Chapter~4 and for first encouraging the author's interest in this area of mathematics. The author also thanks Hunter Hamby for discussions of some of the results. This book was prepared independently: neither of them directed or reviewed it, and neither is responsible for its contents. Any errors are the author's alone. This research was originally motivated by understanding the papers of Okayasu \cite{Okayasu2026} and Kian \cite{kian2026relative}, but then sprawled into a general study of the Arveson--Radon--Nikodym theorem and how this can be used to study norm estimates of channel means. Throughout this process, the works of Bhatia \cite{bhatia1997matrix} and Hiai \cite{hiai2010matrix} have proven invaluable. Once the author noticed the problem with the geometric channel fidelity, stemming from the work of \cite{cree2020fidelity}, the author decided to push just how far this problem could be taken: the continuity estimates, discrimination, and the extension of the results to the hockey-stick divergences. Hence, during the preparation of this work the author used Claude (Anthropic; models Opus~5.5 and Fable~5.1). The purpose of this at first was to help to build a roadmap of the current literature which exists on the subject. Once trained on the material and on what the author wanted to do using the ARN theorem, these models were used in studying the background literature, searching for references, suggesting proofs and statements, and suggesting edits to the exposition. This book would not exist without the fundamental work of Arveson \cite{arveson1969subalgebras}, Raginsky \cite{Raginsky2003}, Kian \cite{kian2026relative}, Okayasu \cite{Okayasu2026}, Cree and Sikora \cite{cree2020fidelity}, Hiai and Mosonyi \cite{HiaiMosonyi2017}, and Watrous \cite{watrous2018theory}. Chapter~4 of this book is the author's arXiv submission \cite{Jones2026}; the rest of this book followed from the author's work with Claude over several months.
